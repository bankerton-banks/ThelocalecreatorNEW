About Us

The Locale Creator is a bi-annual student run magazine at The New School. Each semester we collect creations of all kinds from students and publish their work in the magazine.

History

In 2025, two students came together both noticing something missing from The New School community; a way to see students' work without the boarders of departments, majors, and grade. They wanted to see what students were creating, in a physical form. They were tired of constantly finding inspiration online and sought to create something that would serve as a rescue, for finding that inspiration.

Out of these desires, The Locale Creator was brought to life bridging gaps between students and their visibility of others creations.

Our goals

  • Connect students
  • Highlight creation across various mediums
  • Serve as a source of inspiration
  • Support students goals and practices
  • Create a space for all voices to be heard
